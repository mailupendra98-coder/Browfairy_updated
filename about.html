Meet Ridhima Sony

The Artist Behind Browfairy PMU Studio

At Browfairy PMU Studio, we believe beauty is at its best when it looks effortless. Our philosophy is simple—permanent makeup should enhance your natural features, not change who you are.

Founded by Ridhima Sony, a certified Permanent Makeup (PMU) artist, Browfairy was created with a vision to bring advanced PMU techniques, artistic precision, and uncompromising safety standards to Hyderabad. Every treatment is thoughtfully customized to complement your unique facial features, skin tone, and personal style, ensuring results that are elegant, balanced, and naturally beautiful.

Ridhima specializes in advanced PMU treatments including Nano Brows, Nano Combination Brows, Lip Blush, Dark Lip Neutralization, Lash Line Enhancement, and Brow Corrections. Her approach combines technical expertise with an artist's eye for detail, creating enhancements that look soft, natural, and tailored to each individual client. She continually invests in advanced training and education to stay aligned with global techniques, trends, and safety protocols.

What truly sets Browfairy apart is our commitment to personalization. We understand that no two faces are alike, which is why every procedure begins with a detailed consultation to understand your goals, preferences, and lifestyle. From brow mapping and color selection to treatment design and aftercare, every step is carefully planned to achieve results that feel authentically you.

Our Studio.

Our studio is a blend of visual artistry along with hygiene. We follow strict hygiene protocols and use premium-quality pigments, sterilized equipment, and proven techniques to ensure the highest standards of safety and comfort. We are committed to creating a welcoming, professional environment where every client feels informed, confident, and cared for throughout their beauty journey.

Whether you're looking to wake up with perfectly shaped brows, naturally tinted lips, or subtly enhanced features, our goal is simple—to help you look and feel like the best version of yourself.

Beauty Backed by Professional Excellence

Browfairy PMU Studio is proudly co-founded by an IIM alumnus, bringing together artistic excellence and professional business standards. This unique combination ensures that every aspect of the client experience—from consultation and treatment protocols to hygiene, transparency, and customer care—is delivered with the same commitment to quality and attention to detail that defines our work.

Our Promise

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all beauty. We believe in enhancing what already makes you unique.

At Browfairy PMU Studio, we don't simply perform procedures—we create natural-looking enhancements that inspire confidence every time you look in the mirror.
