Lash Lift and Tint in Hyderabad

CURL AND DARKEN YOUR NATURAL LASHES — NO EXTENSIONS.

Lash lift and tint

Lash Lift & Tint is a low-maintenance beauty treatment that enhances your natural lashes by giving them a lifted, curled, and darker appearance — without the need for extensions or daily mascara. The lash lift gently curls the lashes from the root, while the tint deepens their color for a more defined, eye-opening effect.

This treatment is perfect for anyone wanting naturally longer-looking, fuller lashes with minimal effort.

The result is beautifully lifted and tinted lashes that make your eyes appear brighter, more awake, and effortlessly enhanced — lasting up to 6–8 weeks.

  • Lift and tint tailored to your natural lash length and eye shape.
  • No extensions — your own lashes look curled and darker.
  • Performed with professional lash solutions in a hygienic studio.
  • A low-maintenance option between PMU appointments.

Wake up with lifted, tinted lashes — no mascara required.

Who it is for. Straight or downward lashes that need lift and tint without extensions. Pair with lash liner for more definition, or brow lamination for a full eye grooming appointment.

Aftercare. Keep lashes dry for the first 24–48 hours as advised. Results typically last 6–8 weeks. Serving Kondapur and Hyderabad.
