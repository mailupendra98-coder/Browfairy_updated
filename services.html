Microblading

Microblading

Microblading is a manual technique considered to be semi-permanent makeup or cosmetic tattoo, also known as eyebrow embroidery, microstroking or 3D brows.

Combination Brows

Combination brows blend microblading with a soft, powdered look for a natural yet defined brow.

Ombré Powder Brows

Ombre brows are shaded brows that darken at the tail and lighten at the front, creating a seamless gradient effect.

Lip Neutralization

A specialized treatment to correct dark lips, neutralizing unwanted cool tones and restoring a more balanced lip color.

Lip Blush

A semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo that enhances the natural shape and color of your lips, giving them a youthful, fuller look.

Lash Liner

A subtle, natural-looking permanent eyeliner applied directly into the lash line to create the illusion of thicker, darker lashes.

Nano Brows

An advanced technique using a single needle machine to create very fine, crisp hair strokes that mimic natural eyebrow hairs.

Nano Combination Brows

A combination of nano hairstrokes and machine shading for a textured, realistic, and defined brow look.

Lash Lift and Tint

A semi-permanent treatment that curls and darkens your natural lashes, making them appear longer and more voluminous without extensions.

Brow Lamination and Tint

A treatment that straightens and lifts brow hairs, giving them a fuller, more defined shape, followed by tinting to enhance the color.

Hydrafacials

An advanced non-invasive skin treatment that deeply cleanses, exfoliates, extracts impurities and hydrates the skin for an instantly refreshed, glowing complexion.

Chemical Peels

A professional skin treatment that uses chemical solutions to improve skin texture, tone and clarity by removing damaged outer layers.
